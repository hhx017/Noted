# Usage guide

A quick walkthrough of every screen in Noted BuJo.

## First start

1. Enter your license key to activate the app (contact your administrator if you don't have one).
2. On first launch a school year is created automatically, along with three example subjects. You can rename or delete them under **Faecher verwalten**.

## Dashboard

The dashboard is your daily home screen.

- **Today's card** shows your focus tasks ("grosse Aufgaben") for today. Use *Zum Tagesplan* to open the full day plan.
- **Kommende Termine** lists your next five upcoming appointments (exams, deadlines, private dates).
- **Heutige Habits** lets you check off today's habits directly.
- **Search bar** – type at least 2 characters to search across all tasks, appointments, and module topics. Click a result to jump straight to it.

## Jahresplan (year plan)

- If no school year exists yet, enter a start and end year and generate the year plus its week grid.
- **Monate** – toggle months on/off; click to open the month plan.
- **Wochen** – one button per calendar week; the current week is starred.
- **Ferien** – add holiday periods with name, start, and end date.
- **Schulfreie Tage** – add individual days off school.
- **Termine** – add exams/klausuren or deadlines/abgaben with optional subject. These appear in the PDF and on the dashboard.
- The **schedule icon** in the top right opens your timetable.

## Monatsplan (month plan)

- Calendar grid with dots indicating how many tasks are planned per day.
- **Ueberblick** – monthly goals and overview text.
- **Monatsreflexion** – end-of-month reflection.
- Don't forget to hit *Speichern*.

## Wochenplan (week plan)

- Shows the planning appointment configured for the week.
- **Input-Phasen / Selbsterarbeitungs-Phasen** – lecture and self-study phases.
- **Themen** – module topics for this week.
  - Click a topic, then click a day to assign it to that day.
  - Add topics via *Neues Thema hinzufuegen* (subject + module dropdowns).
- **Private Termine** – personal appointments for this week.
- **Tage** – open any day plan.
- **Wochenreflexion** – reflect on your week.
- **Migration** – moves all still-open tasks of the week into the next week and marks them as migrated (`▶`).

## Tagesplan (day plan)

- **Grosse Aufgaben** – your focus tasks. Bullet Journaling recommends at most 2–3; a warning appears if you add more.
- **Schnellaufgaben** – tasks that take less than 2 minutes.
- Each task has:
  - a checkbox (open ↔ done),
  - a status button (⟳) cycling through `○` open → `●` done → `▶` migrated → `◆` discarded,
  - a delete button.
- Tasks added from the FAB (+) quick dialog land on today's plan.

## Stundenplan (timetable)

- 8 periods × Monday–Friday grid.
- Click any cell to set subject (pick a chip or type your own), room, and teacher.
- Click a filled cell again to edit it; *Loeschen* clears it.

## Habits

- Navigate weeks with the arrows around the week label.
- Click a day box to mark the habit as done for that date.
- Add habits with the + button: name, color, and an optional time window (e.g., 07:00–08:00).
- Today's habits also appear on the dashboard.

## Faecher (subjects)

- Manage subjects: name, color, teacher.
- Inside a subject you can maintain its **module/topic list** — these feed the topic dropdowns in the week plan.

## Index

An automatically maintained table of contents with page numbers for every month, week, and day you have planned. Click an entry to jump there.

## Settings

### Profil
Name, school, graduation target, and optional study info. This appears on the PDF cover page.

### Darstellung
Choose **Hell** (light), **Dunkel** (dark), or **System** theme.

### PDF-Einstellungen
- **PDF-Titel** – title printed on the cover (default: BULLET JOURNAL).
- **Akzentfarbe / Text / Gitter** – colors used throughout the PDF.
- **Gitterstil** – style of the timetable grid.
- **Bereiche** – toggle which sections are exported (cover, TOC, year overview, week plans, habit tracker, timetable).
- **Vorschau** – exports a PDF filled with sample data so you can try layouts without touching your real data.
- **Speichern** persists the settings; the normal export always uses them.

### Daten & Export
- **Plan als PDF exportieren** – renders your complete school year as described above.
- **Alle Daten loeschen** – wipes everything (plans, subjects, habits, profile). Cannot be undone!

## Tips

- Plan weekly: sit down at your planning appointment, review last week's open tasks, use migration, then distribute topics across days.
- Keep focus tasks small — everything under 2 minutes belongs in Schnellaufgaben.
- Use the search instead of hunting through weeks; it indexes every task, appointment, and topic.
